It takes a tribe.

Why we're called Tribe

Children don't learn to communicate in a therapy room. They learn at the breakfast table, on the playground, at bedtime, and in the classroom, from the people who love them most.

Tribe Speech Therapy was built around that idea. We bring therapy into your child's world and work side by side with the whole "tribe": parents, grandparents, siblings, nannies, and teachers. Everyone leaves with simple, practical ways to keep the progress going long after the session ends.

When might a speech evaluation be helpful?

Every child develops at their own pace. Still, many parents reach out when they notice things like:

  • Using fewer words than other children the same age
  • Being hard for others to understand
  • Frustration when trying to communicate
  • Limited eye contact or play skills
  • Repeating sounds or getting "stuck" on words
  • Concerns about social interaction

If you're noticing any of these, an evaluation can help you get clarity and a plan. Only a qualified professional can tell you whether your child would benefit from therapy.

Common questions from parents

Where do speech therapy sessions take place?

Sessions take place in your home, at your child's school or daycare, in community settings like a park, or online by video. We don't see clients in a clinic, so therapy fits into your child's everyday life.

What ages do you work with?

Tribe works with children from toddlerhood through the teen years.
