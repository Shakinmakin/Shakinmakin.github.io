About our services

What does a speech-language pathologist do?

A speech-language pathologist (SLP), sometimes called a speech therapist, evaluates and treats challenges with speech, language, social communication, and related areas. Pediatric SLPs like Elora specialize in working with children and teens.

Where do speech therapy sessions take place?

Tribe doesn't see clients in a clinic. Sessions take place in your home, at your child's school or daycare (with the school's permission), in community settings like a park, or online by video.

What areas do you serve?

In-home, school/daycare, and community sessions are available throughout Beverly Hills, West Los Angeles, and the San Fernando Valley, including Century City, Westwood, Bel Air, Brentwood, West Hollywood, Beverlywood, Cheviot Hills, Santa Monica, Culver City, Sherman Oaks, Encino, Tarzana, and surrounding areas. Online sessions are available to families located anywhere in California.

What ages do you work with?

We work with children from toddlerhood through the teen years.

Do you offer speech therapy in Farsi?

Yes. Elora speaks Farsi and offers evaluations, therapy, and parent coaching in English and Farsi.

Will speaking two languages confuse my child or cause a speech delay?

This is one of the most common worries we hear. According to ASHA, learning two languages does not cause speech or language disorders, and children around the world grow up speaking more than one language. Bilingual children may follow a slightly different path, for example mixing words from both languages, which is a typical part of bilingual development. A careful evaluation looks at a child's skills across all the languages they use, not just English.

What does a speech therapy session look like?

Sessions are play-based and built around your child's interests and personal goals. Parents and caregivers are welcome to participate, and each session usually ends with simple ideas to practice during the week.

Is online speech therapy effective for kids?

ASHA recognizes telepractice as an appropriate way to deliver many speech and language services. Whether it's a good fit depends on your child's age, needs, and attention. We'll talk it through with you and recommend the best setting for your child.

Getting started

How do I schedule?

Call us at (310) 526-3331 or email Elora@tribespeechtherapy.com. We'll talk about your child, answer your questions, check your insurance, and schedule an evaluation.

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

It depends on your insurance plan. Some plans require a referral or prescription from your child's doctor before speech therapy is covered. We can help you find out what your plan requires.

What happens at a speech evaluation?

An evaluation usually includes a conversation with you about your child's development, play-based observation, and age-appropriate testing of speech, language, and communication skills. Afterward, we'll review the findings with you and share recommendations.

How do I know if my child needs speech therapy?

Only an evaluation by a qualified professional can answer that for your child. Many families reach out when their child is using fewer words than peers, is hard to understand, seems frustrated when communicating, stutters, or has trouble with social interaction. You're always welcome to call with questions.

How long does speech therapy take?

Every child is different. The length of therapy depends on your child's needs, goals, and progress. We review progress regularly and keep you informed every step of the way.
