General information only

The content on this website, including text, graphics, milestone guides, and links to other websites, is provided by Tribe Speech Therapy for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be, and should not be used as, a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, evaluation, or treatment.

No professional relationship

Reading this website, calling, or sending an email does not create a therapist-client or provider-patient relationship. A professional relationship begins only after an evaluation has been scheduled and agreed to.

Every child is different

Children develop at different rates. Information about milestones, signs, or common concerns is general in nature and may not apply to your child. Always seek the advice of your child's physician or another qualified health provider with any questions you have about your child's health or development. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on this website.

Emergencies

This website and our phone and email are not monitored for emergencies. If you believe your child is experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Outside links

Every child's progress in speech therapy is different. Tribe Speech Therapy does not guarantee specific outcomes or results.
