Our services

Speech Therapy Services for Kids & Teens

Tribe Speech Therapy provides pediatric speech and language evaluations and individualized therapy for children from toddlerhood through the teen years in Beverly Hills, West Los Angeles, and online throughout California.

Where therapy happens

We don't have a clinic waiting room, on purpose. Sessions take place wherever your child communicates every day.

Most popular

In-Home Speech Therapy

We come to your home in Beverly Hills and the Westside. Your child works on goals with their own toys, family, and daily routines, and you see exactly how to help between sessions.

Learn about in-home therapy

School & Daycare Sessions

With permission from your child's school or daycare, sessions can take place where your child learns, with coordination alongside teachers and staff.

School & daycare visits

Online Speech Therapy (Telehealth)

Live, interactive video sessions for families located anywhere in California. Great for busy schedules, older kids, and parent coaching.

Learn about teletherapy

Community Settings

Parks, playgrounds, and everyday outings give children real-life chances to practice greetings, requests, play, and conversation.

Community sessions

What we offer

Speech & Language Evaluations

A comprehensive assessment of your child's speech, language, and communication, with clear findings and recommendations.

About evaluations

Individual Speech & Language Therapy

One-on-one, play-based sessions built around your child's personal goals and interests, with progress tracked over time.

Parent Coaching

Parents and caregivers learn practical strategies to support communication during everyday routines like meals, bath time, and play.

Bilingual Farsi & English Therapy

Speech therapy in English and Farsi for bilingual and Persian-speaking families.

Farsi speech therapy

School & Team Collaboration

With your consent, we coordinate with teachers, pediatricians, and other providers so your child's whole team works together.

AAC Support

Support for children who communicate with picture systems, speech-generating devices, or apps, and for the families learning alongside them.

About AAC

Areas we support

Tribe works with children on a wide range of speech, language, and social communication goals. Explore general information about each area:

  • Late talkers & early language delays
  • Speech sound (articulation & phonology) challenges
  • Language comprehension & expression
  • Stuttering (fluency)
  • Childhood apraxia of speech
  • Social communication, including autism
  • AAC (alternative & augmentative communication)
