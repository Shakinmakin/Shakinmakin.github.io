About Tribe Speech Therapy

Meet Elora Barsiasa, M.S., CCC-SLP

Pediatric speech-language pathologist and founder of Tribe Speech Therapy, helping children in Beverly Hills and Los Angeles build communication skills and confidence.

A little about Elora

Elora Barsiasa is a pediatric speech-language pathologist (SLP) who works with children from toddlerhood through the teen years. She founded Tribe Speech Therapy to offer families something she believes every child deserves: therapy that feels like play, fits into real life, and treats parents as true partners.

Elora's sessions are individualized, play-based, and family-centered. She starts with what your child loves, whether that's trains, dinosaurs, dress-up, or a favorite video game, and builds communication goals around it. Children stay engaged, and parents see exactly how to keep practicing between visits.

Elora speaks Farsi and offers therapy in both English and Farsi. She understands the questions bilingual and Persian-speaking families often have, and she supports children in the languages that matter to their families.

It takes a tribe to raise a communicator.

Why "Tribe"?

Children learn to talk surrounded by their people: the parents who narrate the grocery trip, the grandparent who sings the same song every visit, the older sibling who asks "what's that?", the teacher who waits for an answer. A child's communication grows inside that circle, their tribe.

We named the practice Tribe because we believe the most meaningful progress happens when everyone in that circle is working together. That belief shapes everything we do:

  • We come to you. Therapy happens at home, at school, at daycare, at the park, or online, where your child actually communicates every day.
  • Families are part of every session. Parents and caregivers are invited to join, ask questions, and learn simple strategies they can use during everyday routines.
  • We collaborate. With your permission, we coordinate with teachers, pediatricians, and other providers so everyone is on the same page.
  • We honor every family's language and culture. Therapy is available in English and Farsi, and we respect the languages spoken at home.
  • Every child communicates in their own way. And at their own pace. We celebrate every step.

What does a session look like?

To your child, it looks like play. Behind the scenes, every activity is chosen to work toward their personal goals.

Play-based activities

Toys, games, books, and movement that keep children engaged and motivated.

Your child's interests

We follow your child's lead so practice feels natural, not like a drill.

Personalized goals

Goals come from a thorough evaluation and are adjusted as your child grows.

Home strategies

Every session ends with simple ideas for the week ahead.
