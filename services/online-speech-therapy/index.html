How online speech therapy works

Online speech therapy (also called teletherapy or telepractice) takes place over a live video call. Your child and the therapist see and hear each other in real time, using interactive games, digital books, drawing tools, and toys you already have at home.

ASHA, the national association for speech-language pathologists, recognizes telepractice as an appropriate way to deliver many speech and language services when it fits the child and the goals. During your first conversation, we'll talk honestly about whether online sessions are a good fit for your child.

Who online therapy can work well for

  • School-age children and teens who are comfortable on a screen
  • Families with busy schedules or long commutes
  • Families outside our in-home service area but within California
  • Parent coaching for younger children, where a parent and therapist work together
  • Bilingual families looking for therapy in Farsi and English

What you'll need

  • A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone (a laptop or desktop works best)
  • A reliable internet connection
  • A quiet space with few distractions
  • For younger children, a parent or caregiver nearby to help

Mix and match

Some families combine online sessions with in-home visits, for example a home visit one week and an online session the next. We'll help you find a schedule that fits your family.

Location requirements

Because speech-language pathologists are licensed by state, online sessions are available to children who are physically located in California at the time of the session.
