Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Tribe Speech Therapy ("we," "us") handles information related to this website, tribespeechtherapy.com.

Information we collect through this website

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts, or online payments, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you contact us by phone or email, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, and your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and to keep the site running. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. Google's handling of that information is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

How we use information

We use the information you send us only to respond to your inquiry, schedule services, and communicate with you. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Email and health information

Regular email is not a fully secure form of communication. Please don't send detailed medical or health information about your child by email before you become a client. Once your child begins services, protected health information is handled according to our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided to clients.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may have rights regarding their personal information under California law. To ask about the information you've shared with us, contact us using the information below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
